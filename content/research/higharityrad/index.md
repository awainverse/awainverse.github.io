#### Abstract

We characterize several properties of higher-arity function classes, specifically finite VC$_k$-dimension, slicewise finite VC$_k$-dimension, and finite slicewise Littlestone dimension, in terms of generalizations of Rademacher complexity.

These characterizations sets up simple new proofs of several results on finite VC$_k$-dimension and slicewise finite VC$_k$-dimension. These include the preservation of finite VC$_k$-dimension under taking expectations and uniform law of large numbers statements for both properties. All of these results apply naturally to real-valued function classes as well as boolean-valued classes.

We also prove a new adversarial uniform law of large numbers for classes with finite slicewise Littlestone dimension.
